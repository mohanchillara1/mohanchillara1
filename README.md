### Brine

![Next.js 15](https://img.shields.io/badge/Next.js-15-30363D?style=flat&logo=nextdotjs&logoColor=white) ![React 19](https://img.shields.io/badge/React-19-298DFF?style=flat&logo=react&logoColor=white) ![SpacetimeDB 2.14](https://img.shields.io/badge/SpacetimeDB-2.14-298DFF?style=flat) ![TypeScript 5](https://img.shields.io/badge/TypeScript-5-3178C6?style=flat&logo=typescript&logoColor=white) ![WebXR](https://img.shields.io/badge/WebXR-Meta_Quest-30363D?style=flat) [![Live demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_demo-brine--hackutd.vercel.app-298DFF?style=flat)](https://brine-hackutd.vercel.app)

A night-shift operations console for oil-field produced-water sites. It finds failing pumps before their tanks trip, plans the cheapest response across a shared truck fleet, and flags sensors whose readings disagree with physics. Built with two teammates at HackUTD 2026 for EOG Resources' challenge.

[Live demo](https://brine-hackutd.vercel.app) · [Code](https://github.com/yajatp/hackutd26v2)

### GR00T N1.7 research

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat&logo=python&logoColor=white) ![PyTorch](https://img.shields.io/badge/PyTorch-EE4C2C?style=flat&logo=pytorch&logoColor=white) ![NVIDIA GR00T N1.7](https://img.shields.io/badge/NVIDIA-GR00T_N1.7-76B900?style=flat&logo=nvidia&logoColor=white) ![LIBERO](https://img.shields.io/badge/Benchmark-LIBERO-30363D?style=flat)

I evaluated NVIDIA's GR00T N1.7 generalist robot policy under degraded training data, with a peer researcher. Paper: *Training Data Quality and Novel-Object Transfer Under Visual Degradation: A Study of GR00T N1.7* (2026). Advised by Dr. Aravind Chiruvelli (Walmart Global Tech), with a methodology review by Dr. Alice E. Smith (University of Alabama).
